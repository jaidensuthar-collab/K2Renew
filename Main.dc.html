What a lease actually commits you to.

Eight things, in the order most landowners ask.

Who holds the deed
You do. Your name stays on the deed.
How long the lease runs
Lease term missing
k2renew.com does not state one anywhere. K2 Renew to supply. No number goes here until they do.
What happens at the end
Every lease includes decommissioning and restoration clauses requiring removal of equipment and return to original condition.
Farming or grazing meanwhile
The development phase typically lasts 2 to 5 years. During it, most land stays usable for farming, grazing or hunting.
If the lease is cancelled
The land reverts to full use and you keep any payments already made.
If you sell the land
The lease transfers with the property. The new owner honors the existing terms.
Who pays the costs
The developer handles all costs, permitting and upkeep. You are never asked to cover costs.
When payments start

During the development phase, before construction. They increase to the full rate once the site is operational.

Lease rates vary by location, infrastructure access, and market demand. In most cases, energy leases provide higher and more stable income than traditional farming or ranching.
